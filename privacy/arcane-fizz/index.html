PRIVACY & DATA

Arcane Fizz
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy describes how Lohkforged Studios ("we", "us") and third-party services process information when you play Arcane Fizz, a casual Android puzzle game.

1. Developer and contact

Arcane Fizz is developed by Lohkforged Studios in Australia. Our website is lohkz.github.io. Privacy enquiries can be sent to lohkforgedstudios@gmail.com.

2. Gameplay and local storage

The game does not require you to register a player account or supply your name, email address or postal address to play. It stores game progress (including the highest unlocked level) and sound/music preferences locally on your device. When available, purchases of additional moves may also create locally saved move vouchers and a record of fulfilled purchase identifiers to avoid granting the same purchase twice.

Local save data can be lost if you clear application storage or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup arrangements. We do not currently operate a player account, cloud save or custom gameplay database. Local consumable move vouchers are not recoverable from Google Play after they have been consumed and acknowledged.

3. Optional advertising (Google AdMob)

Arcane Fizz is designed to offer optional rewarded video advertisements which players may choose to watch in exchange for extra moves. The game does not currently require you to watch an ad to continue playing or restart a level. Ads are only requested where the installed version enables advertising and applicable consent requirements are satisfied.

Google AdMob and advertising partners may process your device and app characteristics, internet protocol (IP) address, advertising identifiers (where available), approximate location derived from network data, ad engagement and diagnostic information for ad serving, measurement, fraud prevention and, where allowed, personalisation. For details see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. We may receive aggregate advertising statistics.

4. Optional in-app purchases (Google Play)

Where enabled, Arcane Fizz offers optional purchases of additional moves through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment information under its own privacy policy; we do not directly obtain your card number or payment credentials. The game may receive product, transaction and purchase-status information from Google Play to grant purchased items, prevent duplicate fulfilment, and respond to payment failures or deferred purchases.

Purchased extra-move vouchers are consumable. After fulfilment and acknowledgement, Google Play generally cannot restore consumed items automatically following reinstallation. Purchases must never be assumed successful based merely on opening or closing the Google Play purchase screen.

5. Consent and privacy choices

When relevant, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may request advertising consent or show information about privacy choices before ad requests are made. Availability of consent and privacy-option controls depends on location, legal requirements, Google settings and the installed version of the game. You may also manage Google advertising settings using My Ad Center and your device's privacy settings.

6. Third-party processing and retention

Google and its service providers may process or retain relevant information in countries outside Australia, subject to their policies and applicable legal safeguards. Information held by Google is controlled by Google's retention and deletion practices. You can delete locally saved gameplay information by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to device backups.

7. Children and families

The game does not ask players to register an account or disclose personal details. Advertising and age-related configurations are subject to the declared Google Play target audience and applicable requirements. Parents or guardians can contact us about privacy concerns using the email address above.

8. Security and policy changes

We use local application storage for basic gameplay records and rely on Google's tools for advertising and purchase processing. No digital service can promise absolute security. This policy may change when the game's features or services change; updates will be posted here with a revised date.

Website note: This static privacy page does not itself include advertising or analytics scripts.
