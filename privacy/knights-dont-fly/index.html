PRIVACY & DATA

Knights Don't Fly
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how Lohkforged Studios ("we", "our") handles information in Knights Don't Fly, our Android mobile game, and how third-party advertising services may use information.

1. Who we are

Knights Don't Fly is developed by Lohkforged Studios, an independent game developer based in Australia. Our official website is https://lohkz.github.io/. For privacy questions, see the contact section below.

2. Information stored by the game

The game does not require an account or ask you to submit your name, email address or other contact details to play. The game stores some information locally on your device, including:

  • Your best score and related gameplay progress needed for the high-score display.
  • Your music, sound effects and vibration preferences.

These settings are stored using the device's application storage. We do not currently operate a player-account server or an online high-score database. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app generally removes locally held preferences, subject to any backup or restore functions provided by your device or operating system.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The game is designed to use Google AdMob to display occasional interstitial advertisements between completed runs. Advertisements are not intended to interrupt active gameplay. AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, advertising identifiers (when available), device and app information, ad interactions, approximate location inferred from network information, and diagnostic or performance data. This information may be used for ad delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement and, where permitted, personalised advertising.

Google's collection and processing practices are described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising. We may receive aggregate ad performance reports, rather than an individual player's identity.

During development, test advertisements may be used instead of live ads. The practices described in this section apply when advertising services are enabled in a distributed version of the game.

4. Optional Remove Ads purchase

Where available, Knights Don't Fly offers an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase using Google Play Billing. Google Play processes payments, payment methods, purchase confirmations and associated transaction records under Google's Privacy Policy. Lohkforged Studios does not directly receive or store your full payment-card details.

To recognise and restore the purchase, the game may receive purchase status and product identifiers from Google Play and store a local flag indicating that automatic advertising has been removed. Previously owned non-consumable purchases can be restored through the same Google Play account, subject to the store's policies and successful verification. We may use aggregate purchase information provided by the store for accounting and product support.

5. Consent and advertising choices

Where required by applicable privacy rules, the game requests advertising consent or offers applicable privacy choices using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can choose whether to consent to relevant processing when a consent notice is shown. Where a privacy-options entry point is required, it is provided in the game's Settings screen, subject to the installed version.

You can also manage advertising preferences through your Android device's privacy or advertising settings and Google's My Ad Center. Withholding consent may affect the types of ads available but should not prevent ordinary retries. Third-party privacy choices may also be governed by Google's own terms and controls.

6. Third parties and international processing

Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related information on servers in different countries, in accordance with their own policies and legal safeguards. We do not operate these third-party systems. Please review Google's linked policies for further details about processing, storage and retention.

7. Data retention and your choices

Locally saved settings and scores remain on the device until they are cleared, reset or removed, subject to device backups. Advertising information held by Google or its partners is subject to those providers' own retention and deletion processes. You can use the privacy tools in the app (where provided), Android settings and Google's privacy controls to exercise available choices.

8. Children's privacy

We do not ask players to register an account or directly provide identifying information. Advertising and age-related protections depend on the game's declared audience and applicable laws. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern, please contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to limit the information the game itself collects and store basic gameplay preferences locally. No digital service can guarantee absolute security. Third-party advertising systems are operated and secured by their respective providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect game updates, new services or legal requirements. The latest version will be published at this address with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For general support, privacy enquiries, or to exercise applicable privacy rights, please email Lohkforged Studios at lohkforgedstudios@gmail.com. We will review enquiries and respond as appropriate.

Website note: This page is served as a static website. It does not itself embed advertising or analytics scripts.
